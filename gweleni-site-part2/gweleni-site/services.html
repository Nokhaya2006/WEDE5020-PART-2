What We Do

Services

Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Which areas do you work in?

We are based in Bellville and mainly work across Cape Town’s northern suburbs, including Durbanville, Brackenfell, Parow, Tyger Valley and Century City.

Do you provide free quotes?

Yes. Send us your project details through the quote request page and we will get back to you with next steps.

How long does a typical renovation take?

It depends on scope: a bathroom renovation usually takes two to three weeks, while a kitchen renovation or home extension can take several weeks to a few months. We’ll give you a realistic timeline as part of your quote.

Do you manage the whole project, or just the building work?

We manage the full project — planning, scheduling and quality control — from the initial quote through to handover, so you deal with one team throughout.
