About Us

Two decades of hands‑on craftsmanship

Gweleni Construction is a Bellville-based construction company established in 2006. We specialise in residential building, renovations, extensions and roofing for clients across Cape Town’s northern suburbs — including Durbanville, Brackenfell, Parow and Tyger Valley — from first-time homeowners to small commercial operators.

We have built our reputation on strong client relationships and careful, hands-on workmanship rather than volume — every project is managed from planning through to handover by the same team, so nothing gets lost between site visits and quotes.

Our Vision

To be the northern suburbs’ most trusted construction partner, recognised regionally for quality, reliability and professionalism.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality construction projects on time and within budget, building long-term client relationships through transparent communication and skilled workmanship.
